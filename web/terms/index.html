2. Description of Cherry Scheduler

Cherry Scheduler is web-based software that helps schools create and optimize timetables for teachers and classes. It lets schools enter classes, teachers, subjects, teaching assignments and scheduling rules, generates timetables using constraint-based scheduling and optimization techniques, and lets users review, edit and export timetables.

Generated timetables depend on the data and rules provided by the school. Some combinations of rules cannot all be satisfied at the same time; in those cases the software may leave lessons unscheduled or report rules it could not meet. Schools remain responsible for reviewing and approving the timetables they use.
