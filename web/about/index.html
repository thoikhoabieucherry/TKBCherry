About Cherry Scheduler

Cherry Scheduler is a Vietnamese EdTech startup founded in 2026. We build school timetable optimization software designed to reduce the complexity and manual effort involved in creating schedules for teachers and classes.

The problem we work on

School timetabling is a constraint optimization problem involving teacher availability, class conflicts, subject requirements, teaching workloads, session limits, and many school-specific rules.

Cherry Scheduler is being developed to make this process faster, easier to manage, and more accessible to school administrators.

What Cherry Scheduler does

  • Lets schools enter classes, teachers, subjects and teaching assignments in one place.
  • Captures scheduling rules such as teacher availability, subject requirements, session limits and school-specific constraints.
  • Generates feasible timetables with a constraint-based scheduling and optimization engine, then improves timetable quality by reducing unnecessary teacher sessions and gaps.
  • Lets administrators review and adjust timetables and export them to Excel or Word.
